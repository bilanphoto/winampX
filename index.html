Features

  • Authentic Winamp 2.91 Engine: 275px modular windows with magnetic snapping.
  • Resizable Playlist: Drag the bottom-right corner of the Playlist window to resize it to any height!
  • Skins: Classic 2.91 (Original Titanium) and Modern Silver Blue.
  • Persistence: Audio files and playlists are saved automatically to browser IndexedDB and restored across reloads!
  • Equalizer: Genuine 10-band BiquadFilter equalizer (-12dB to +12dB) with Preamp and 16 presets.
  • Visualizer: Real-time 60FPS frequency spectrum analyzer with falling peak dots and oscilloscope!
